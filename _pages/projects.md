<h2 class="cv-section">TiDES-RM: Reverberation Mapping with 4MOST and LSST</h2>

<p class="lead"><strong>Role:</strong> Co-lead (2025 –) &nbsp;·&nbsp; <strong>Status:</strong> survey starting 2026; simulation paper in preparation</p>

<p>TiDES-RM is the reverberation mapping program of <strong>TiDES</strong>, the 4MOST Time-Domain Extragalactic Survey (<a href="https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/adff4e">Frohmaier et al. 2025, ApJ, 992, 158</a>). Over five years, it will repeatedly observe about 1000 broad-line AGNs with the 4MOST multi-object spectrograph, while the Vera C. Rubin Observatory's Legacy Survey of Space and Time (LSST) provides densely sampled multi-band continuum light curves of the same objects. Combining the two makes it possible to measure broad-line region sizes and black hole masses for a large AGN sample spanning a wide range of redshift and luminosity.</p>

<h3>Survey design</h3>

<div class="cv-item"><div class="cv-main"><strong>Fields</strong><br><span class="cv-sub">Four 4MOST pointings (~4.2 deg² each) inside the LSST Deep Drilling Fields: ELAIS-S1, XMM-LSS, ECDF-S and COSMOS</span></div><div class="cv-date"></div></div>
<div class="cv-item"><div class="cv-main"><strong>Sample</strong><br><span class="cv-sub">~900–1000 broad-line AGNs at 0 &lt; z &lt; 4, down to r ≈ 20.5 mag, selected from Milliquas, DES DR2, SDSS and HELM variability</span></div><div class="cv-date"></div></div>
<div class="cv-item"><div class="cv-main"><strong>Spectroscopy</strong><br><span class="cv-sub">4MOST low-resolution spectrograph, 3700–9500 Å, R ≈ 4000–7700; 1 h per epoch, roughly every 7–14 days in grey time</span></div><div class="cv-date"></div></div>
<div class="cv-item"><div class="cv-main"><strong>Photometry</strong><br><span class="cv-sub">LSST Deep Drilling Field ugrizy light curves every 2–3 days, extended back in time with HELM (DECam) and VST archival monitoring</span></div><div class="cv-date"></div></div>
<div class="cv-item"><div class="cv-main"><strong>Baseline</strong><br><span class="cv-sub">Five years of coordinated spectroscopic and photometric monitoring</span></div><div class="cv-date">2026 – 2031</div></div>

<h3>Science goals</h3>

<ul>
<li>Measure Hα, Hβ, Mg II and C IV time lags over a wide range of redshift and luminosity</li>
<li>Calibrate radius–luminosity relations and single-epoch black hole mass estimators</li>
<li>Velocity-resolved reverberation mapping of the brightest and most variable sources</li>
<li>Track changing-look AGNs and nuclear transients discovered during monitoring</li>
</ul>

<h3>Current work: simulating TiDES-RM</h3>

<p>In <em>TiDES Reverberation Mapping Project. I. Simulating the Outcomes from Five-Year Monitoring</em> (Wang et al., in preparation), we build end-to-end simulations of the survey: mock continuum light curves from a damped random walk, broad-line responses from a finite-width transfer function, sampling with realistic field-by-field LSST and 4MOST schedules, magnitude-dependent photometric and spectroscopic noise, and lag recovery with interpolated cross-correlation. The simulations quantify how lag recovery depends on source brightness, lag, variability amplitude, cadence, seasonal gaps and survey baseline, and guide the survey design choices that matter most for the science return.</p>
