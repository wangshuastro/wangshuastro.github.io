<h2 class="cv-section">TiDES-RM: Reverberation Mapping with 4MOST and LSST</h2>

<p class="lead"><strong>Role:</strong> Co-lead (2025 –) &nbsp;·&nbsp; <strong>Status:</strong> survey starting 2026</p>

<p>TiDES-RM is the reverberation mapping program of <strong>TiDES</strong>, the 4MOST Time-Domain Extragalactic Survey (<a href="https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/adff4e">Frohmaier et al. 2025, ApJ, 992, 158</a>). Over five years, it will repeatedly observe about 900 broad-line AGNs with the 4MOST multi-object spectrograph, while the Vera C. Rubin Observatory's Legacy Survey of Space and Time (LSST) provides densely sampled multi-band continuum light curves of the same objects. Combining the two makes it possible to measure broad-line region sizes and black hole masses for a large AGN sample spanning a wide range of redshift and luminosity.</p>

<h3>Survey design</h3>

<div class="cv-item"><div class="cv-main"><strong>Fields</strong><br><span class="cv-sub">Four 4MOST pointings (~4.2 deg² each) inside the LSST Deep Drilling Fields: ELAIS-S1, XMM-LSS, ECDF-S and COSMOS</span></div><div class="cv-date"></div></div>
<div class="cv-item"><div class="cv-main"><strong>Sample</strong><br><span class="cv-sub">~900 broad-line AGNs at 0 &lt; z &lt; 4, down to r ≈ 21.5 mag</span></div><div class="cv-date"></div></div>
<div class="cv-item"><div class="cv-main"><strong>Spectroscopy</strong><br><span class="cv-sub">4MOST low-resolution spectrograph, 3700–9500 Å, R ≈ 6000; 1 h per epoch, roughly every 7–14 days in grey time</span></div><div class="cv-date"></div></div>
<div class="cv-item"><div class="cv-main"><strong>Photometry</strong><br><span class="cv-sub">LSST Deep Drilling Field light curves in multiple bands (ugrizy), with griz sampled every 2–3 days</span></div><div class="cv-date"></div></div>

<h3>Science goals</h3>

<ul>
<li>Measure Hα, Hβ, Mg II and C IV time lags over a wide range of redshift and luminosity</li>
<li>Calibrate radius–luminosity relations and single-epoch black hole mass estimators</li>
<li>Velocity-resolved reverberation mapping of the brightest and most variable sources</li>
</ul>

<h3>Current work: simulating TiDES-RM</h3>

<p>In <em>TiDES Reverberation Mapping Project. I. Simulating the Outcomes from Five-Year Monitoring</em> (Wang et al., in preparation), we build end-to-end simulations of the survey. With the help of LSST photometry, the 4MOST spectra can be calibrated to a precision of 1–2%, and the simulations predict that lags can be successfully recovered for a large fraction of the sample. In particular, the survey will deliver a large number of AGNs with lags measured in multiple emission lines, such as Hβ + Mg II and Mg II + C IV. TiDES-RM will also enable studies of broad-line region kinematics and dynamical modeling for a large sample across different emission lines. Together, these will lead to better constraints on the radius–luminosity relation, broad-line region kinematics and black hole mass measurements.</p>
